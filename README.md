## 📊 Dataset

Quarterly Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) data for 2024:

| Quarter | CAC (USD) |
|---------|-----------|
| Q1      | 223.40    |
| Q2      | 225.27    |
| Q3      | 230.78    |
| Q4      | 230.93    |
| **Average** | **227.6** |

**Industry Target CAC:** 150  

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. The company’s average CAC (227.6) is **77.6 units higher** than the industry benchmark of 150.  
2. CAC has shown a **consistent upward trend** from Q1 to Q4:
   - Q1 → Q2: +1.87  
   - Q2 → Q3: +5.51  
   - Q3 → Q4: +0.15  
3. The **largest jump** occurred in **Q2 → Q3**, indicating increasing inefficiencies in marketing spend.

## 💡 Business Implications

- Rising CAC directly increases the cost of acquiring each customer, reducing **profit margins**.  
- If the trend continues, customer growth will become **unsustainable** relative to competitors.  
- High CAC may also indicate **overspending on underperforming channels** (e.g., ads with low conversion).  

---

## ✅ Recommendations

To align with the industry target of 150, the company should:

1. **Optimize Digital Marketing Channels** (primary solution):
   - Reallocate spend toward **high-conversion channels** (e.g., organic search, referrals).  
   - Reduce investment in **low-ROI campaigns**.  
   - Use **A/B testing** to improve ad effectiveness.  

2. **Leverage Data-Driven Insights**:
   - Implement advanced **attribution models** to track performance across touchpoints.  
   - Use **predictive analytics** to forecast CAC trends.  

3. **Improve Customer Retention**:
   - Strengthening retention reduces pressure on acquisition, indirectly lowering CAC.
